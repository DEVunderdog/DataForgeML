# C2ST scores the table, not the imputer — an independent evaluation module

Evaluation asked the wrong question. `EvaluationOrchestrator.inspect` reported a variance ratio a human had to eyeball, and `score_accuracy` cross-validated every model-based column to produce cell-level RMSE, MAE and R² — a grade of the *imputation model*, paid for with a 5× refit. The literature is unambiguous that this is the wrong target: FIMD §2.6/§3.2.3 rejects cell-level error outright, and Näf et al. (Fig. 8) rank the **oracle** imputer *last* by RMSE. An imputer that predicts the conditional mean everywhere wins on RMSE while destroying the variance and every dependence the table had.

The imputation model is disposable and is never scored. What is scored is whether the resulting **table** carries the distribution and dependence structure of the data that produced it. The first metric that answers that question is a **Classifier Two-Sample Test** (Lopez-Paz & Oquab, arXiv:1610.06545v4): train a classifier to tell filled rows from observed rows, and read its held-out accuracy as a standardised `z` against the closed-form `Binomial(n_te, ½)` null. If nothing can tell them apart, the filled data is distributionally indistinguishable from the real data.

It lives in a new top-level `dataforge_ml/evaluation/` package, with `evaluation/imputation/` as its first consumer, because evaluation targets will multiply (encoding, scaling, downstream fidelity) and C2ST is generic by construction — it takes two labelled samples and never sees a `FittedImputer`. The old surface is deleted outright with no deprecation path: this is a pivot, not a patch.

## Status

accepted — supersedes ADR-0057, ADR-0058 and ADR-0059. Depends on ADR-0085 (the dtype floor), and the new record types are born compliant with ADR-0086 (the Rendering Contract).

## The construction

- **Column-conditional, and the row-level joint variant is rejected.** For column `c`, pile A is the rows where `c` was observed and pile B the rows where `c` was filled; the classifier sees **whole rows**, not just `c`. Rejected: per-column *values* — a one-feature classifier is a marginal test wearing a costume and is perfectly gamed by a marginal-matching imputer (Shadbahr et al. Fig. 1). Rejected: a row-level joint test with pile A as the fully-observed rows — `(1−p)^k` decay puts pile A at **1.2%** of rows at 20 columns and 20% nulls, the geometric width cliff #436 measured and #435 locked as "abandoned, not narrowed". Column-conditional pile A is `n·(1−p_c)` and does not depend on table width at all.

- **No single table-level number is produced.** If one is wanted it comes from Energy Distance, which has no complete-row requirement.

- **The target column `c` is itself a feature.** A median fill puts every filled cell on one exact value — the most common imputation failure there is, and invisible if `c` is hidden. Expected and correct consequence: scalar strategies (Mean/Median/Mode/Constant) fail C2ST at ~100% detection every time. That is the *baseline* that makes a model-based number interpretable; #435's audit flagged that scalar strategies get no diagnostic at all today.

- **Balancing by repeated subsampling is mandatory.** Unequal piles break the `Binomial(n_te, ½)` null outright: at 850 vs 150 a classifier that always answers "A" scores 85% having learned nothing. The larger pile is subsampled to the smaller pile's size and the whole test repeated `R` times with different draws. Rejected: balanced accuracy with a permutation null — it discards the closed-form null, which is the entire reason C2ST was chosen.

- **Pool by the mean `z` against the single-run null, on both repeated axes. Dividing the null's SD by `√R` or `√m` is forbidden** — it manufactures significance. The `R` repeats share pile B entirely; the `m` frames share pile A entirely, since the mask is identical in every frame. Measured (#487): the correlation between two repeats' `z` is only **0.09**, so the rule is conservative rather than merely safe — it costs power it does not need to. Neither level's field is ever named plain `z`: `C2STResult.mean_repeat_z` is the mean over `R`, `C2STScore.mean_frame_z` the mean over `m`, and both carry a spread beside them.

- **Train/test scheme is a user dial; balancing is not.** `split` (1 fit per repeat) vs `cross_validation` (5 fits per repeat) is a compute-budget choice and both keep `n_te` exactly known. Default `split`: repeats are already mandatory, and a default that silently costs 5× is a bad default. `R` is never auto-downgraded when CV is on.

- **The `z` is the score; the p-value is never the score.** A p-value is a function of the discrepancy *and* of `n`, so it changes meaning across datasets and floors out. The hypothesis test survives only in the human-facing sentence. The p-value is **one-sided, upper tail** — accuracy below chance is noise, not evidence of good imputation, and a two-sided p halves the power for nothing.

- **Multiple comparisons: Benjamini–Hochberg on the verdict, nothing on the score.** `k` columns is `k` tests, so ~1 spurious failure per run at 20 columns. The raw `z` stays uncorrected because it *is* the score; BH controls the false discovery rate for "which columns should a human go look at". Bonferroni rejected: brutal at `k=20`, and its independence assumption is wrong for correlated columns.

- **Overfitting fails safe and is guarded anyway.** A memorising classifier scores at chance on held-out rows, so it yields false *negatives*, never false positives — confirmed at train accuracy 1.000 against test 0.497, with null FPR 3.7–6.9% at every pile size. Two guards survive: train accuracy is reported beside test accuracy, so a 100%/50% split reads as "uninformative" rather than clean; and the spread of the `R` z-scores is reported, not just their mean. A third guard — forcing `early_stopping` on — was **overturned by measurement**: it does not prevent memorisation (0.87 train against 0.62 test at ~300 rows per pile) and costs real detection (degenerate imputer 100% → 89% at 250 rows per pile).

## The floor, set by measurement

`binomtest` is gone from C2ST entirely. Enumerating `Binomial(n_te, ½)` exactly, the normal rule's true size never exceeds 5.9% anywhere in `10 ≤ n_te ≤ 400`, and at several `n_te` it selects the *identical* cutoff — the exact test buys at most one lattice step. The real failure is the classifier: HGB's default `min_samples_leaf=20` cannot split fewer than 40 training rows, so it constant-predicts, scores exactly 0.5 against a balanced test set, and reports `z = 0.000` — **a constant fill returned as a perfect pass.** Three tiers, because there are three different failures:

1. **`min_samples_leaf` defaults to 5.** Measured not to break the null (z SD 0.92–1.04, FPR 1.5–4.5% at every pile ≥ 30) and to cure the constant-predictor pathology outright.
2. **A hard refusal floor, `min_filled_cells = 30`, applied to `min(n_observed, n_filled)`** — the *smaller* pile, checked before balancing. Every number ever measured here was measured in rows per pile after balancing; naming pile B specifically left a hole where a 5-observed / 500-filled column ran a five-row test and reported a `z`. FPR is 9.0% at 20 per pile and 4.5% at 30.
3. **Low power is annotated, never refused, up to `low_power_below = 250`.** The degenerate imputer is caught 9% of the time at 60 rows per pile, 40% at 100, 81% at 150, 100% at 250. Between 30 and 250 the test is honest but weak, and a pass there must read "not enough data to tell", not "clean". Refusing outright below 250 sounds safer and would gut the feature.

A **runtime degeneracy check** — every repeat's classifier predicting a single class — backs this up and is explicitly *not* the floor: at 30 rows per pile with `min_samples_leaf=20` the classifier is not yet constant and still detects only 6%. It is useless without being visibly broken.

## The classifier, and the two pins

`HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, injectable. It fits a polars DataFrame directly, reads `pl.Categorical` via the `categorical_features='from_dtype'` default and handles nulls natively — so there is no pandas, no pyarrow and no encoder for the library to own and defend. `RandomForestClassifier` was rejected as the *default* because it cannot eat text and because grown-to-purity is the saturation-prone end of Snoke's failure mode 2; it stays perfectly usable through injection.

**The pinning rule, which is the durable part:** the library pins a classifier setting only where sklearn's default is *structurally wrong for balanced C2ST piles* — that is, where the default encodes an assumption about dataset size. It does not pin settings that merely express a tuning preference. Exactly two qualify:

- **`min_samples_leaf = 5`, fixed and never scaled to pile size.** A scaled leaf floor would make two columns in one report products of different-capacity models, and since the `z` *is* the score, that makes the number a function of the column's missingness rate as well as its distributional damage.
- **`early_stopping = False`, pinned rather than inherited as `'auto'`.** `'auto'` resolves to `n_samples > 10_000` on the training rows of one balanced repeat — `min(pile) > ~7,150` at a 70/30 split. Inheriting it gives two different algorithms in one report with nothing in the output saying so, and it cuts capacity on exactly the piles with the most power.

`max_iter`, `learning_rate`, `max_leaf_nodes`, `l2_regularization` and `class_weight` were all checked and are inherited: none is tuned against a row count, and raising them is *tuning the classifier*, which is what the injection channel is for.

**This breaks a repo precedent and the break is deliberate.** `_STRATEGY_DIALS` states that every imputation dial value is also the sklearn default of the estimator it reaches. C2ST is the first place the library overrides a sklearn default. Those dials are decide-time formulas degenerating to neutral inputs; these two pins are corrections to an assumption about `n` that C2ST structurally violates.

The pins live in a **generic-side factory**, `_default_c2st_classifier`, reading one module-level constant that is also `C2STConfig.min_samples_leaf`'s field default. Without it, `c2st(classifier=None)` would build a bare sklearn-default HGB — a gap neither the record ticket nor the seam ticket owned, since one made `min_samples_leaf` a config dial and the other ruled that the config never crosses the seam.

**Injection is total.** An injected instance is used verbatim, with no `set_params` reach-in — precedent: `custom_estimators` holds the user's live instance and is not even cloned (ADR-0083). Reaching in breaks on any classifier lacking the parameter and needs a `try`/`except` that silently does nothing, which is the worst outcome available: the user cannot tell whether their setting survived.

## The seam

One generic function, private under ADR-0050:

```python
def c2st(reference, candidate, *, repeats, scheme, classifier=None,
         random_state=None, emitter=None) -> C2STResult
```

- **Two frames, not a frame plus a label vector.** Which pile is which becomes structural: you cannot pass a `y` of the wrong length, in the wrong row order, or with a third value in it. Balancing with two frames is one line; with a label vector you partition, subsample and rebuild the labels. The names `reference` and `candidate` carry the meaning at the cost of implying an asymmetry the arithmetic does not have — accuracy does not care which pile is which.
- **The generic side owns balancing, the `R` loop and the size floor** — the three things that make the arithmetic valid. If balancing sat adapter-side, a consumer passing unequal piles would get a number that looks exactly like a valid `z` and is meaningless. `repeats` and `scheme` cross the seam as plain arguments; `C2STConfig` never crosses it.
- **The generic side knows nothing about `m`.** Multiple imputation is an imputation concept; a second consumer has no analogue and an `m` parameter would sit at 1 forever. The `m` loop and `mean_frame_z` are the adapter's. The one piece hoisted generic-side is the *pooling rule*, because "mean z against the single-run null, never divide by `√k`" is exactly the rule people get wrong and it deserves one home and one docstring.
- **There is no column-type description — dtype is the contract.** A parallel type argument would be a second source of truth for a fact sklearn reads off the frame anyway. A `pl.String` column raises a named error identifying the column; an int-coded categorical sitting as `pl.Int64` is silently treated as an ordered numeric and is a stated, unenforceable precondition on the caller's typing.
- **The size floor is exposed as a pure predicate the adapter calls first**, and asserted internally so it cannot be bypassed. On a wide table refusal is the *common* path — 190 dense columns emit 190 refusals — and raising 190 exceptions to describe the ordinary case is control flow standing on its head.
- **The adapter owns, exhaustively:** resolving active columns, deriving the mask via `_resolve_effective_nulls`, the `Text`/`Identifier` gate, choosing and ordering the columns, building the two piles, the `m` loop and frame pooling, the refusal floors and annotations, Benjamini–Hochberg, and assembling the report. Everything not on that list is generic.

Validated against two imputation-free consumers — train/test drift (reference = training rows, candidate = holdout) and outlier clipping (reference = rows left alone, candidate = rows clipped). Both are served with zero changes, and neither has anything resembling an `m` axis.

## The records

One record per **active** column, always: *untestable is a result, never an absence.* `report[col]` and `col in report` survive the `m` axis because the mask is `m`-invariant, so the `m` frames nest *inside* the column record rather than becoming a second key. Rejected: a flat list or a dict keyed by `(column, frame_index)` — both repeat every mask-derived fact `m` times, and nothing in the type says the copies must agree.

**Every number that only exists when the test ran lives inside one nullable `score` object.** `result.score.mean_frame_z` on a refused column raises `AttributeError` on the spot; it cannot propagate a `0.0` or a `None` into a mean, a plot or a comparison. A refused column and a chance-scoring column differ by *having or not having a score object at all* — the outcome enum alone does not achieve that, because `mean_frame_z: float | None` beside it would reintroduce the arithmetic collapse in a new costume.

The outcome vocabulary is closed at six members — `Tested`, `NoFilledCells`, `NoObservedCells`, `TypeNotTestable`, `BelowSampleFloor`, `Uninformative` — with two annotations riding on a `Tested` result (low power, pile imbalance) and two per-frame flags (degeneracy, unseen category). The verdict is **three-valued** (`Flagged` / `NotFlagged` / `NoVerdict`) and stamped onto the record by the report after BH runs: a refused column has no verdict, and `False` would read as "checked, fine". 

**The three outcome enums are exported, and this is a stated exception to ADR-0050.** `C2STOutcome`, `C2STAnnotation` and `C2STVerdict` classify output and are never supplied by the user, which is the exact test that keeps `TypeFlag` internal. They are exported anyway, because reading this report *is* comparing against them — `rec.outcome is C2STOutcome.Tested`, `rec.verdict is C2STVerdict.Flagged` — so the *handling* half of the rule reaches them where it does not reach a flag a user only ever prints. The refined boundary: an output enum is exported when the value drives the caller's control flow, and a three-valued verdict exists precisely to be branched on. Given up: the user-supply test is no longer the whole rule, so "is it an input?" must now be followed by "and does the caller branch on it?" — a second question, and a fuzzier one. The alternative, forcing a submodule import on the primary read path of the feature, taxes every correct use to keep one sentence tidy.

`C2STProvenance` hangs off the report — one classifier and one config per call — and records the classifier's **full `get_params()`, stringified, not its `repr`**, because sklearn's `repr` elides exactly the parameters you need when arguing with a report.

## The entry point

```python
def evaluate_imputation(original_df, imputed_frames, profile, *, config,
                        metrics=None, observer=None) -> EvaluationReport
```

- **A free function, not an orchestrator class.** The map already ruled the entry point stateless, and a class whose only instance state is the observer is constructor ceremony around a single call. `observer=` rides the call and never touches `EvaluationConfig` — the reason the observer is kept off configs is that a live callable breaks their serializable, setter-only contract (ADR-0044), and a keyword argument satisfies that reason exactly as well as a constructor does. Matches where the library has been moving: ADR-0071 collapsed the imputation orchestrator, ADR-0072 collapsed persistence.
- **It takes the profile and the config, not just two frames.** The charted two-argument form cannot work: `_resolve_effective_nulls` needs the profile's sentinels, the `Text`/`Identifier` gate needs `SemanticType`, and the Phase Active-Columns Contract needs `PipelineConfig`.
- **Umbrella on all three sides.** An umbrella name over an umbrella config returning a metric-specific record would be two-thirds of a design. `EvaluationReport.c2st: C2STReport | None` is the first and today only field; Energy Distance later becomes `report.energy_distance`, purely additively. `metrics=` selects, defaulting to `None` meaning *every metric the library has*; `report.c2st is None` means **not requested**, deliberately a different state from *ran and refused*. Rejected: a nullable nested config (`EvaluationConfig.c2st = None` means off) — it makes absence of configuration and refusal of the metric the same sentinel, and puts a control-flow switch inside a declarative config.
- **`metrics=` does not violate accuracy-over-speed.** That principle governs computations feeding a *library* decision: profiling computes every indicator always because routing gets better with each one. Evaluation metrics are independent answers to different questions handed to a *human*; running one does not make another's answer more correct. Narrowing here is scope selection on an opt-in diagnostic, not staging a decision to save cost.
- **`EvaluationConfig` stands alone and never touches `PipelineConfig`**, with `C2STConfig` nested inside it so Energy Distance lands as a sibling. `PipelineConfig` is built once for a run; evaluation is opt-in, stateless and re-run repeatedly with different knobs while poking at results.

## Observability and concurrency

- **Observability follows loop ownership.** ADR-0074 stripped Events from `fit_unit` because *the caller driving the loop already knows where it is* — a condition on who owns the loop, not on which phase you are in. Evaluation is one call after which the library iterates columns × frames × repeats internally, so the position-in-the-call is not the user's to know. `StructuralProfiler` keeps Events for the identical reason. Written down in exactly those words, because otherwise two idioms sit side by side and the split looks arbitrary.
- **`item` is the column, `substep` is the repeat.** The frame is the wrong Substep floor: at `m=1` it emits one Substep per column — a duplicate of the `item` event — while the `R` fits, the entire long stretch, stay silent. The repeat can only be emitted from inside the generic `c2st`, which is fine: `Emitter` takes `phase` and `stage` as plain strings and is already phase-agnostic infrastructure, and the seam's rule bans *imputation*-shaped knowledge, not observability.
- **`Emitter` gains `stage_start()` and `stage_end()`, purely additively.** Those two lived only on `_ObservabilityMixin`, a *class* mixin, and a free function can inherit nothing. `_ObservabilityMixin` is left exactly as it is.
- **`phase="evaluation"` as a bare string, with no new `PipelinePhase` member; `stage="c2st"`.** `PipelinePhase` enumerates the six sequential phases and its job is feeding `resolve_active_columns`; evaluation is opt-in, out of band, and resolves its columns against `PipelinePhase.Imputation` — it evaluates *the imputation phase's* column set. Naming the stage after the metric means later metrics land as sibling stages.
- **Refused columns emit their `item` and nothing else.** `total` is the count of active columns, so a refused column that emitted no `item` would leave the bar short of its own total, reading as a hang. A `decision` event carrying the refusal reason was rejected: the reason is already on the record, and duplicating it into the stream creates two renderings of one vocabulary that can drift. **The event stream is progress only.**
- **No pool anywhere, and that is not a compromise.** `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` has no `n_jobs`: it parallelises over OpenMP and takes the whole box on every fit. An outer pool would add no parallelism to an idle machine and would oversubscribe one the inner fits already saturate — the failure ADR-0081 exists to arithmetic its way out of. All three loops are plain sequential `for` loops, so ADR-0075 and ADR-0081 need no amendment, there is no `core_budget` analogue and no `max_workers` dial, determinism is free, and **the library now owns zero pools**.
- **No `FitSignals` analogue.** `FitSignals` exists because `fit_unit` returns an artifact that outlives the call, so how-the-fit-went could not be attached without becoming a stale claim. Evaluation returns no artifact: the report *is* the record of how the run went.

## Excluded columns, and why the obvious argument is wrong

Evaluation resolves `PipelinePhase.Imputation`'s active columns and both tests and builds features from that set only. It honours the contract on **governance** grounds, not fairness ones — and the fairness argument must not appear in this ADR, because it does not survive contact with the code. There is no single set of columns "the imputer saw": a Median fill saw none, GroupWise saw one, ClusterConditional saw the correlated features, KNN saw its block, MICE saw the block plus every active numeric column. Mirroring that set would hand a Median-filled column zero features, and would stop C2ST ever detecting that filling `income` destroyed its relationship with `city`. **C2ST is supposed to see more than the imputer saw** — that asymmetry is the entire source of its power. Predictor selection is a modelling decision that binds nothing outside its unit; exclusion is a *scope declaration*, and the Phase Active-Columns Contract (ADR-0077) binds every phase entry point to honour it. Given up: real damage visible only through a deliberately excluded column goes undetected, with no dial to widen the test.

**100%-null columns are dropped from the feature matrix** when testing every other column — such a column is pure imputer output with no observed anchor, so it can contribute noise or leak the imputer's own structure and nothing else. The rule is specifically 100%-null and **not** "untestable": a `NoFilledCells` column is entirely observed data and is the most trustworthy feature in the table. And it stops at the endpoint — a 50%- or 60%-missing column stays a feature, because at 60% it still carries 40% real observed data, and any cut between is arbitrary.

## Consequences

- **Attribution blur is a stated consequence, not a bug.** A heavily-but-not-fully imputed feature carries the imputer's fingerprint, so a fired test names the column whose *filled-ness* is detectable, not necessarily the column imputed badly; correlated missingness spreads one fault across a group. The `z` is true; the attribution is not. Unfixable inside C2ST — separating "which column is damaged" from "which column's filled-ness is detectable" is what **Per-Column W₁** is for.
- **The MAR caveat is printed and not suppressible.** A fired test is ambiguous between "the imputation is bad" and "the missingness is strongly MAR", because under MAR the two piles are *supposed* to differ (FIMD §6.6.2); only Propensity-Conditional Comparison separates them. It is a fixed footer whenever at least one column is `Flagged`. There is deliberately no dial: the full-configurability principle governs thresholds and behaviour, and a switch whose only function is to stop the library disclosing a known limitation of its own number makes the report say something untrue.
- **An injected bare HGB is worse than the default, and the guard does not cover it.** Injection being total means an injected `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` at sklearn defaults reintroduces the constant-predictor pathology at 30–60 rows per pile — precisely where the degeneracy check was measured to be unreliable. Only the `classifier_injected` flag in provenance warns the reader. A documented hole, not a designed-away one.
- **Scalar strategies fail at ~100% by design.** Anyone reading a report will see every Mean/Median/Mode column `Flagged`. That is the baseline, and the report's framing must make it read as such rather than as 40 defects.
- **A table handed over cold cannot be evaluated.** You must have kept the profile. The deleted `score_accuracy` already took one, so this is not a new burden.
- **Evaluation configures differently from every other phase**, breaking ADR-0030's Phase Sub-Config topology deliberately.
- **`phase` on a Pipeline Event is now sometimes a `PipelinePhase` value and sometimes a bare string**, so the field's domain is looser than it looks. Recorded in `CONTEXT.md` so `"evaluation"`'s absence from the enum does not read as an oversight.
- **`_c2st.py` imports from `observability`**, so the generic module is no longer importable in isolation; lifting C2ST into a standalone package would carry the event spine with it.
- **A closed six-member outcome enum is a versioned surface** — a seventh member is a breaking change. And a `ColumnC2STResult` lifted out of its report carries a verdict whose meaning depends on the set it came from.
- **Breaking removals, no deprecation path:** `EvaluationOrchestrator` (the class, not merely its methods), `InspectionReport`, `InspectionDiagnostic`, `AccuracyReport`, `AccuracyDiagnostic`, `score_accuracy`, `inspect` *the orchestrator method* (which **clears** its clash with the exported persistence `inspect`), `_MODEL_BASED_STRATEGIES` — C2ST inverts its premise by testing every active column — and both `NumericImputationConfig.refit_r2_cv_folds` and `refit_r2_min_complete_rows`, which load silently from old serialized configs rather than erroring.
- **`k_capped` is promoted to no typed field.** It exists nowhere today, being computed inside `evaluation.py` from the fitted model against the frame's height; the KNN fitter's existing `knn_params:` note is extended instead, because `FitSignals`' typed core is for facts a consumer *branches* on. Given up: it stops being machine-readable.
- **`m > 1` is accepted by the surface from day one and produced by nothing.** The dividing line is stochastic vs deterministic, not model-based vs scalar — MICE with `sample_posterior=False` is model-based and produces byte-identical frames. Producing `m > 1` is a sibling map.
